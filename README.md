## What I'm building

- **BailSetu** — Custody-time tracker + bail draft assistant for undertrials under S.479 BNSS: triage engine, reasoning in plain language, draft bail applications, hash-chained audit log, English/हिन्दी views. Built for MY Bharat Hack for Social Cause 2027.
- **LegalAid India** — Pan-India legal aid eligibility checker under Section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act: three eligibility questions first, then one printable application for the nearest DLSA office. Live at [vishwajeetsah.github.io/legalaid-mp](https://vishwajeetsah.github.io/legalaid-mp/).
- **Machine learning from scratch** — k-means, CART decision trees & random forests, collaborative-filtering recommenders, time-series forecasting — all in NumPy.
- **LLM tooling** — mini-rag (RAG Q&A over your own documents, no API key needed), llm-eval-kit, prompt-playground.
